## Geometry becomes computation

Magnitude describes size, direction captures relative proportions, and a dot product combines aligned components. Models use transformations to map input vectors into representations that make a task easier.

## Scale matters

Distance can be dominated by a feature with a much larger numeric range. Inspect units, normalize when the model requires it, and keep preprocessing identical between training and inference.
