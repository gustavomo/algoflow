Staff engineering is a scope of impact, not a reward for writing more code. Your job is to improve an important outcome through technical judgment, alignment, and systems that continue working without you in every decision.

<Hint>
  Start with the business or user constraint. Technology matters because of the
  outcome it changes.
</Hint>

## Find consequential work

Look beyond the loudest request. Map the objective, the teams and systems involved, the cost of leaving the problem alone, and the evidence that would show improvement. A Staff engineer makes problem selection explicit before committing an organization to a solution.

## Create leverage

Leverage can come from an architecture decision, a safer delivery mechanism, a reusable platform, a clearer operating model, or engineers who can now make better decisions independently. The common property is durability: the improvement survives beyond your direct involvement.

## Calibrate your scope

Use four questions: Is the outcome important? Does it cross a meaningful boundary? Are the trade-offs ambiguous? Will the result improve future decisions or delivery? A large project without these properties may still be valuable, but size alone does not make it Staff work.
