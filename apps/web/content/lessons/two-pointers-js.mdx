Two pointers coordinate two indices under an invariant. A common version starts at both
ends of a sorted array and moves the side that cannot participate in the answer.

<Hint>
  Before moving a pointer, state what remains true about the region you skipped.
</Hint>

## Example

For a target sum, compare `values[left] + values[right]`. Move `left` when the sum is too
small and `right` when it is too large. Each pointer moves at most `n` times.
