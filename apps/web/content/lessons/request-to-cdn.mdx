<Hint>
  A CDN is a cache boundary near users, not a replacement for the origin.
</Hint>

## Trace delivery

DNS directs the client toward an appropriate edge. The edge checks its cache. A hit avoids an origin round trip; a miss fetches from the origin, returns the response, and may store it according to cache policy.

## Design with evidence

State what can be cached, for how long, and how invalidation works. Then measure hit ratio and tail latency. A CDN improves a specific delivery path; it does not automatically fix slow dynamic work.
