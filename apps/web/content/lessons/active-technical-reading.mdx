Technical reading becomes learning when it changes what you can explain or do without the page open.

<Hint>
  Notes are inputs to recall and application, not a second copy of the book.
</Hint>

## Read with a question

Preview headings and examples, then write the question you expect the section to answer. Read for that model. Close the material and reconstruct the answer before checking what you missed.

## Convert reading into practice

Write one compact synthesis, one retrieval prompt, and one small application or counterexample. Schedule the prompt for later review. Confidence without retrieval is not yet evidence of learning.
